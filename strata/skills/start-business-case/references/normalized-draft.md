# Build the normalized draft

1. The draft has two member-supplied values: the brief `title` and the brief `description`. Intake accepts no separate framing fields for a new case; supply an internal nonblank `changeReason` behind the scenes in tool calls without asking the member.
2. Use the member's own words. You may infer a concise `title` from the description when none was supplied; disclose the inference. Do not invent business facts.
3. `title` is a short noun phrase naming the case (at most 200 characters). `description` states the decision the case supports and any objective or success criterion supplied (at most 2000 characters). Preserve supplied measures, thresholds, uncertainty, and attribution exactly.
4. Do not ask separate questions for a decision question, counterfactual, materiality statement, deadline, constraints, alternatives, implementation dates, or why the member is starting the case; leave unanswered framing details unset until post-creation editing. When the member volunteers extra details (such as reasons for starting the case, constraints, deadlines, or alternatives), keep relevant facts inside `description` when they fit within its 2000-character limit (ask one focused question to shorten if over 2000 characters), and tell the member in plain language that extra details can be added after the case is created.
